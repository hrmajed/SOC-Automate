# INC-2025-0212: Invoice-themed macro loader in Finance

## What happened
A payroll analyst on WKS-FIN-011 opened `Invoice_Overdue.docm` delivered from a look-alike billing domain.
WINWORD.EXE spawned `powershell.exe -nop -w hidden -enc ...` which downloaded a second stage from
45.137.21.9 (C2 / staging). Persistence was a scheduled task disguised as a OneDrive helper. Within 10 minutes the
operator dumped LSASS with `comsvcs.dll MiniDump` and harvested a cached helpdesk credential.

## Why Finance is targeted here
Finance workstations regularly have privileged IT staff log in interactively to fix payroll software, which leaves
reusable credentials in LSASS memory.

## Containment that worked
1. Isolate the host through the EDR network containment.
2. Reset every credential that had an interactive session on the host in the last 7 days.
3. Block 45.137.21.9 and the look-alike sender domain at the proxy and mail gateway.
4. Hunt for the scheduled task name and loader hash fleet-wide.

## Lessons learned
Time from macro execution to credential theft was under 10 minutes. The initial alert (Office child PowerShell)
was triaged 3 hours later, after the credential had already been reused.
